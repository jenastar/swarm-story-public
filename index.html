What this is

A character-driven, completely true retelling of the July 2026 OpenAI–Hugging Face agent-swarm incident, built for cybersecurity professionals and general readers alike. Every event is traceable to a cited source: OpenAI's incident technical report, Hugging Face's forensic timeline, and the independent METR / Redwood investigation. No exploit method is ever shown — only what happened, who did it, and why it matters.
